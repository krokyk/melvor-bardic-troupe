# mod.io listing

## Title

Kroky's Bardic Troupe

## Slug

krokys-bardic-troupe

## Summary

Build a full troupe of hired bards in [Myth] Music and enjoy their passive bonuses together.
Buy additional slots in order, with a configurable Music level requirement and GP price.

## Description

**Kroky's Bardic Troupe** expands [[Myth] Music](https://mod.io/g/melvoridle/m/myth-music) with a full ensemble whose passive bonuses work together.

- Hire multiple bards, with configurable slot prices and level requirements.
- Unlock extra mastery rewards and optional skill preservation and item doubling (OFF by default).
- Manage your troupe through a compact roster with enhancement shortcuts.
- Add **The Conductor** for major combat boosts and a **third active prayer** when upgraded.
  Requires **Music level 118** and **Throne of the Herald**.

**This is a very powerful mod, especially with The Conductor: expect a major boost to skilling and combat.**

Requires **[Myth] Music**.
Export your save before disabling or removing the mod.

## Upload configuration

- Upload **bardic-troupe-2.3.2.zip** with `manifest.json`, `setup.mjs`, `config.mjs`, `troupe.mjs`, `conductor.mjs`, `mastery.mjs`, `bonuses.mjs`, `ui.mjs`, `ui.css`, and `icon.svg` at the archive root, plus `assets/conductor.png`.
- Exclude tests and documentation from the archive.
- Set **[Myth] Music**, mod.io ID **3096259**, as a hard dependency in mod.io.
  The current Melvor manifest loader does not read dependency declarations from `manifest.json`.
- Link the Creator Toolkit build to the installed mod.io listing before testing reload and cloud-save persistence.
  Unlinked Toolkit imports can lose additional bard state after reloading.

Browser layout checks pass with Music's markup, Melvor's styles, and 15 mock bards at viewport widths of 1280, 768, 390, and 320px.
